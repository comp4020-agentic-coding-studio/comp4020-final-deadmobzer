## Stat-block legend

Each unit card carries: **MV** (move), **HP**, **DF** (defense dice threshold,
e.g. `3+`), **ARM** (armor type: PHYS/MAG/SUPER/–), **ACT abilities** (name, tag,
range, effect), **Traits**, and **three per-type upgrades** (exclusive — pick one).
MV HP DF ARM appear as a 4-column header row just under each unit name.
