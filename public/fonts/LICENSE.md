# Fonts

Self-hosted, both under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (freely redistributable):

- **Pirata One** — https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Pirata+One (display / blackletter)
- **Oswald** — https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Oswald (condensed; stat labels, nav)

They stand in for the rulebook's Metal Blackletter and Impact. Georgia (body) is a
web-safe system font and is not bundled.
